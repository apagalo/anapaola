# Cover Letter Template — Senior Design Operations

> Adapt for each application. Replace [brackets] with specifics. Keep to one page.

---

Dear [Hiring Manager / Team],

I'm writing to express my interest in the [Role Title] position at [Company]. As a Senior Design Operations professional with 3+ years of experience building and scaling DesignOps at Electrolux Group, I bring a unique combination of operational leadership, data-driven decision making, and cross-functional coordination — exactly the foundation that strong design organizations need to deliver at scale.

**What I bring:**

At Electrolux Group, I built the design operations function from the ground up. Starting as a Design Projects Coordinator, I created centralized project tracking and Power BI dashboards across three global design portfolios (Wellbeing, Small Domestic Appliances, and Brand Expression). I then grew into leading design operations — designing workflows, managing budgets and resource allocation, and facilitating cross-functional alignment across distributed teams. Today, as Senior Design Operations, I drive operational strategy for a global design organization.

**Why [Company]:**

[1-2 sentences connecting her experience to the company's specific needs, design team size, product complexity, or stated challenges. Research the company and customize this paragraph every time.]

**What makes me different:**

My path to DesignOps started in marketing and business administration, which gives me a perspective that most operations professionals lack. I speak the language of both design teams and business leadership. I don't just optimize processes — I connect design operations to business outcomes with data, clear communication, and strategic thinking.

I'd welcome the opportunity to discuss how my experience scaling DesignOps at a global consumer electronics company could translate to [Company]'s design organization. I'm based in Stockholm and open to [on-site/hybrid/remote] work.

Best regards,
Ana Paola Galvez Lopez

## Customization Guide

| Section | How to customize |
|---------|-----------------|
| Opening paragraph | Swap role title and company name |
| "What I bring" | Keep as-is — this is the core pitch |
| "Why [Company]" | Research the company. Mention their design team, recent product launches, or DesignOps challenges if public |
| "What makes me different" | Keep as-is — this is the differentiator |
| Closing | Match the work arrangement to the job posting |

## Tips
- Keep it under 350 words total
- Match the tone to the company (startup = more casual, enterprise = more structured)
- If applying through a recruiter, the cover letter can be shorter and more direct
- Always reference something specific about the company — generic cover letters don't work
