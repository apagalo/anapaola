## Must-Have Skills

### Experience
- 5-8+ years in DesignOps, design program management, or related ops role
- Managing complex, multi-track projects across global/distributed teams
- Familiarity with design and research workflows

### Hard Skills
- Project/program management tools (Jira, Asana, Linear)
- Design tool ecosystem (Figma, FigJam, Miro, Confluence)
- Process design and operational roadmaps
- Budget and vendor management
- Data and analytics for operational decisions
- Design systems governance

### Soft Skills
- Change management
- Influencing without authority
- Strong communication (written, verbal, visual)
- Systems thinking
- Facilitation

## Nordic Resume Best Practices

- **Length**: 1-2 pages on A4 (2 pages standard for experienced professionals)
- **Tone**: Collaborative language — "lagom" principle (just right, not boastful)
- **Photo**: Acceptable but not required
- **Language**: English fine for international/tech companies
- **References**: Don't list — "Available upon request"
- **Cover letter**: Important in Sweden, expected for most applications
- **Design**: Minimal, clean, substance over style

## What Hiring Managers Look For in DesignOps Portfolios

| Assessment Area | What They Want |
|----------------|----------------|
| Process thinking | Can you diagnose systemic problems and design scalable solutions? |
| Change management | Have you shipped processes people actually adopted? |
| Cross-functional influence | How did you get buy-in from eng, product, leadership? |
| Metrics and impact | Delivery speed, satisfaction scores, reduced rework |
| Decision rationale | Why this approach over alternatives? |
| Scale and complexity | Team-level or org-level operations? |

### Case Study Format
1. Context — org size, team structure, challenges
2. Diagnosis — root cause identification
3. Approach — systems/processes implemented and why
4. Execution — change management, buy-in, resistance
5. Results — before/after metrics
6. Learnings — what you'd do differently
